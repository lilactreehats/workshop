About this site

It's been an interesting few years in my life. I wont bore you with the story but long story short I ended up with a lot of free time on my hands so I started davesplantcatalogue.net organize my plants. Over the past few years its grown into a more expansive catalog and now I'm trying to find and catalog every plant I can in Ontario and maybe one day across Canada too (I would love to get to see the mountains one day). But yeah its a passion and hobby for me and its been fun

I'll also update old entries whenever I find better notes or photograph or little identification detail. So yeah pages will still occasionally change as a learn new things.

If you find any cool plants message me and see if I can identify what they are.

— Dave
